- *Proof that payroll is right:*
  - **New test, full month:** every active Main employee has the same monthly salary in both halves; the 1st half pays exactly half, and the 2nd half settles the month (Paula ₱25,000, Ana ₱18,000, Bea ₱30,000): pass.
