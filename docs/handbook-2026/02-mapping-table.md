## Needs the school's decision (preliminary; recommended default in brackets)

1. **School name** for the handbook: the brief says "The Shepherd School"; the system says "Shepherd Angels Christian School of Antipolo, Inc." [use the registered name]
2. **Lateness**: today it is recorded but **not deducted** (both late settings are off). [Recommend: deduct late minutes at 100 % of the hourly rate, the same way undertime is deducted, or keep it off and say so plainly]
3. **Leave notice for sick and emergency leave**: the system requires 7 days ahead for every whole-day leave. [Recommend: allow sick and emergency leave to be filed after the fact, within 2 days of returning; needs a system change, listed as a mismatch]
4. **Solo Parent, VAWC and Special Leave for Women** are off in the system but required by law. [Turn them on]
5. **Probation for teachers**: system 6 months; private-school rules use 3 consecutive school years for teachers. [Keep 6 months for staff; teachers per the Manual of Regulations]
6. **Payday**: is pay released on the payslip day (15th / last day) or later? [Same day]
7. **Grace period**: default 10 minutes, schedules 15. [One value for everyone: 15]
8. **Early Bird amount** (live ₱300) and **subsidy amount** (live ₱6,000 marked DEMO). [Confirm the real amounts]
9. Mission, vision, values, statement of faith; dress code; meal break; evaluation; discipline offenses and penalties; HMO; training funds; school-year calendar.
10. **Unused leave conversion** is on in the system; the code says "pending the policy manual". [Confirm, with the 10 tax-free days]
11. **Paternity / maternity days** are marked "pending policy manual" in the system. [Use the legal minimums]

## System vs handbook mismatches found so far

1. Lateness is never deducted with the live settings (see decision 2).
2. Whole-day sick / emergency leave must be filed 7 days ahead (decision 3).
3. Three mandatory statutory leaves are off (decision 4).
4. The old substitution settings (₱150 a period, "fixed") are still stored; the code pays substitution as overtime at 125 %.
5. The retired Perfect Attendance rate (₱500) is still in the rates table, unused.
6. The old "Leave-With-Pay days = 6" setting is still stored; the leave pool (5 / 3 days) is what counts.
7. No way for an employee to dispute a payslip inside the system.
8. Probation is 6 months for everyone (decision 5).
9. The live database still holds test values (daily rate ₱900, subsidy ₱6,000 "DEMO", schedule profiles, payroll sheet approver name); the handbook will state the rules, not these amounts.
