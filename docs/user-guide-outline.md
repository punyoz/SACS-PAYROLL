# User guide outline (per role)

An outline for the staff guides; each heading becomes a short page with
screenshots. Menu names match the portals. Every role's guide starts with
**Signing in** and ends with **Getting help**.

## Everyone: signing in

1. Open the payroll address; sign in with username or email and password.
2. Enter the 6-digit code emailed to you (valid 5 minutes; Resend after 60
   seconds; 5 wrong codes ask for a new one).
3. First sign-in with an issued password: choose a new one (8+ characters,
   upper and lower case, a number and a symbol).
4. Forgot password: "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page (emailed code).
5. Signed out after 60 idle minutes, or when you sign in somewhere else.
6. Profile: your details, emergency contact, change password.

## Employee

- **Dashboard**: this period's attendance and latest payslip at a glance.
- **Attendance**: your taps per day; what On Time, Late, Undertime, Half Day,
  Absent, Incomplete and Holiday mean; asking for a correction.
- **Timesheet**: hours per period.
- **Payslips**: the two payslips a month (1st half pays half the salary with
  no deductions; the 2nd half settles the month); reading each line;
  download PDF.
- **Leave**: filing a request with proof; with and without pay; cancelling.
- Tapping in and out at the RFID terminal: green = accepted, red = tap again,
  amber = saved, will be sent when the internet is back.

## Accountant (own branch)

- **Dashboard**; **View Attendance** (read-only).
- **Process Payroll**: the generation day and the 5-day window; Held
  employees (AWOL / Separated); unresolved attendance blocks an employee;
  Final payslips are locked.
- **Payroll Records** and **Payslips**: finding, printing, PDF.
- **Loans**: encoding a loan (amortization per 2nd half), partial deduction
  and extension, suspended loans, subsidy advances.
- **Incentives & Overload**; **13th Month Pay** (from December 1);
  **Cash Advances** (read-only history, moved to Loans).
- **Subsidy Adjustments**: requesting a missed-month adjustment.
- **Final Pay**: computing it for a separated employee.
- **Payroll Monitoring** and **Payroll Reports** (incl. Payroll Sheet, School
  Format, printed in landscape; Holiday Work).

## HR (all branches)

- **Dashboard**: expiring licenses, recent activity.
- **User Management**: adding an employee or accountant (RFID card number,
  salary, government IDs, emergency contact, licensed-teacher fields and PRC
  verification); archiving (never deleting).
- **Transfer Requests**.
- **Attendance**: monitoring every branch; corrections; overtime approval;
  declaring a suspension (typhoon / LGU).
- **Leave Approval**.
- **AWOL Cases**: the flag, calling, 1st and 2nd notices, recommendation.
- **Approvals**; **HR Reports**.

## Administrator (own branch)

- **Dashboard**.
- **Attendance**: today's board, corrections, overtime; opening the **RFID
  terminal** (unlock with your password each morning; exit with it).
- **Approvals**: AWOL separation decisions, loan decisions, subsidy
  adjustments.
- **Branch Reports**; **Audit Logs** (your branch).
- **System Maintenance**: recording a tap by hand, voiding a lost card.

## Super Admin

- **SA Dashboard**; **Attendance** (all branches).
- **Admin & HR Accounts**: adding, editing, archiving staff accounts.
- **Branch Management**; **Roles & Permissions** (view the matrix).
- **System Configuration**: see [super-admin-settings.md](super-admin-settings.md).
- **Audit & Monitoring**; **System Maintenance**; **Backup & Recovery**.
- Generating a held payslip for a returned employee (Payslip override).

## Getting help

- Can't sign in, or no code arrives: your branch Administrator (staff) or the
  Super Admin (Administrators and HR).
- A wrong tap or a missing time-out: ask HR or your Administrator for a correction.
- A payslip looks wrong: the Accountant of your branch.
